After the keyword research and competitive analysis, you just spent 3 hours writing a great blog for your website – with a 10-minute snack break and two 5-minute potty breaks, of course. You click Publish and off it goes into the world wide web for everyone to read and to be archived for centuries to come. As one of the best blog posts you’ve ever written, this could potentially bring in a lot of traffic and a lot of money.

So I ask this question- how will your blog bring in a lot of traffic? When will it get crawled?

Not Finished Yet!

Maybe sharing on ALL those sites is a bit of overkill, maybe not. You can be the judge there. But did you know there are hundreds of websites out there that are dying to display your content? Oh yes, they are begging to know your blog exists – but you just have to tell them it’s there. We have a little trick that you can do to alert all the search engines and sites that your blog is ready to be crawled. Here’s how you do it:

Log into your WordPress admin, go to Settings, then Writing, then paste the following list in the Update Services box. That’s all there is to it!
