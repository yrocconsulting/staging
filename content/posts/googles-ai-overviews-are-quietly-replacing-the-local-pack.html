Google’s AI Overviews are changing how local businesses appear in search results. Instead of showing the traditional Local Pack, the map and three business listings: Google is increasingly displaying AI-generated summaries at the top of the page. These AI Overviews analyze business websites, reviews, and Google Business Profile data to recommend options directly to users. For many discovery-based searches, this means fewer map listings, fewer direct calls, and less predictable visibility. Local businesses that rely solely on Local Pack rankings may already be losing exposure, even if their SEO hasn’t changed. Understanding this shift is now essential for protecting local search visibility.

TL;DR – What’s Changing in Local Search

  • Google AI Overviews are appearing at the top of search results for many local queries.
  • These AI summaries can replace or push down the Local Pack, reducing map visibility.
  • AI Overviews analyze websites, reviews, and Google Business Profile data to recommend businesses.
  • Fewer listings are shown, which can mean fewer calls and clicks for local businesses.
  • Local SEO now requires authority, clarity, and consistency, not just map rankings.

This shift marks a fundamental change in local SEO, where authority and clarity matter as much as proximity and reviews.

For years, local SEO had a clear goal:
get into the Local Pack.

If your business showed up in that map with three listings at the top of Google, you were winning. Calls came in. Directions were clicked. Leads happened.

But something has changed, and if you’re a local business owner, marketer, or agency, you’ve probably felt it before you’ve clearly seen it.

Google’s AI Overviews are starting to replace the Local Pack for many searches, and it’s reshaping how local visibility works.

The Local Pack Used to Be the Goal

Let’s rewind for a second.

Search something like “best plumber near me” or “roofing company in Dallas” and Google would almost always show:

  • A map

  • Three local businesses

  • Phone numbers, hours, reviews, and directions

That Local Pack was prime real estate. Ranking there meant real business impact.

But Google isn’t just a search engine anymore, it’s becoming an answer engine.

Enter Google’s AI Overviews

AI Overviews are Google’s way of answering a question before you even click.

Instead of showing a map and listings, Google now sometimes shows:

  • A written summary of the “best options”

  • One or two recommended businesses

  • Supporting context pulled from multiple sources

And in many cases, this AI Overview shows above everything else, even above the Local Pack.

So… Is Google Really Replacing the Local Pack?

Short answer: sometimes, yes.

For discovery-based searches like:

  • “Best landscapers near me”

  • “Top coffee shops in Austin”

  • “Best HVAC companies in Phoenix”

Google increasingly prefers to show an AI summary instead of jumping straight to a map.

That means:

  • Fewer businesses are visible

  • Fewer direct calls and clicks

  • Less control over who gets featured

And the businesses shown aren’t always the ones that ranked best in Maps.

Why Google Is Doing This

From Google’s perspective, this makes sense.

Users aren’t always ready to call a business. Often, they’re comparing, researching, or just exploring options.

AI Overviews let Google:

  • Answer questions instantly

  • Reduce friction in search

  • Keep users engaged on the results page

The problem? Local businesses built their entire strategy around map visibility, and that foundation is shifting.

Who’s Feeling This the Most?

Not every search is affected equally.

AI Overviews show up most often for:

  • Non-urgent local searches

  • “Best of” or comparison queries

  • Research-driven searches

Emergency or high-intent searches (“emergency plumber near me”) still commonly show the Local Pack, but even that line is starting to blur.

The Hidden Impact on Local Businesses

This change isn’t just cosmetic. It’s structural.

Fewer Clicks

If users get their answers from AI, they don’t always click through.

Fewer Calls

No map = fewer tap-to-call actions.

Less Predictability

Ranking well in Google Maps doesn’t guarantee AI visibility.

In other words, traditional local SEO signals alone aren’t enough anymore.

What Local SEO Looks Like Now

Local SEO isn’t dead, but it’s no longer just about proximity and reviews.

AI Overviews pull from:

  • Business websites

  • Reviews across platforms

  • Directory listings

  • Structured content and FAQs

  • Brand mentions across the web

Google is asking a different question now:

“Which businesses does the web trust enough to summarize?”

How to Position Your Business for AI Overviews

Here’s where the opportunity is.

Write Content That Actually Answers Questions

AI loves clarity.
Pages that explain services, pricing, processes, and FAQs clearly are far more likely to be referenced.

Keep Your Business Information Consistent Everywhere

AI pulls signals from multiple sources. Inconsistent data weakens trust.

Strengthen Your Authority, Not Just Your Rankings

Mentions, reviews, and well-structured content matter more than ever.

Optimize for Humans First (AI Will Follow)

Ironically, content written clearly for humans performs better with AI than content stuffed with keywords.

The Big Shift No One’s Talking About

The biggest change isn’t that the Local Pack is disappearing.

It’s that Google is deciding which businesses are “worthy” of being summarized, not just listed.

That’s a different game.

And the businesses that adapt now, by focusing on authority, clarity, and AI-friendly optimization, will have a serious advantage over those still chasing yesterday’s rankings.

Final Thought

If your local SEO strategy still revolves entirely around “getting into the map,” you’re already behind.

Google’s AI Overviews aren’t coming, they’re here.

The question isn’t if this affects your business.
It’s whether your business will be one of the few that AI chooses to mention.

What are Google AI Overviews?

Google AI Overviews are AI-generated summaries that appear at the top of search results. They pull information from multiple sources across the web to answer a query directly on Google, often before a user clicks any website.

Are AI Overviews replacing the Google Local Pack?

In many cases, yes. For certain “discovery” searches (like “best roofers in ___” or “top dentists near me”), AI Overviews can appear above the Local Pack or reduce how often the Local Pack shows.

Why is Google showing AI Overviews for local searches?

Google wants to deliver faster, more complete answers, especially when people are comparing options. AI Overviews help Google summarize “best choices” without requiring users to open multiple listings or websites.

Will AI Overviews reduce calls and leads for local businesses?

They can. When AI Overviews show instead of the Local Pack, users may see fewer tap-to-call buttons, fewer map interactions, and fewer direct listing clicks, depending on the query and how Google formats the results.

How does Google decide which local businesses appear in AI Overviews?

AI Overviews appear to favor businesses with strong “trust signals,” including consistent business information across the web, reputable reviews, clear website content, and brand mentions on authoritative sources.

Is traditional local SEO still important?

Yes, but it’s evolving. Google Business Profile optimization and reviews still matter, but businesses now also need strong website content, clear service pages, and consistent authority signals across platforms.

What should I do to increase my chances of being featured in AI Overviews?

Focus on:

  • Clear service + location pages (written to answer real questions)

  • Consistent NAP (name, address, phone) across directories

  • Strong reviews on key platforms

  • FAQs that match how people search

  • Structured data (schema) to help Google understand your content

Do I need to change my website content because of AI Overviews?

If your content is thin, vague, or purely promotional, yes. AI systems prefer clear, structured pages that explain services, who you help, where you serve, and what people should expect.

Can small businesses still compete in AI Overviews?

Absolutely. In many markets, strong clarity and consistency beats “big brand” size. Smaller businesses that publish better answers and maintain consistent presence across the web can show up more often than expected.

Are AI Overviews the future of Google Search?

All signs point to yes. Google continues expanding AI-driven experiences, meaning visibility will increasingly depend on being “AI-understandable” and widely validated across the web.
