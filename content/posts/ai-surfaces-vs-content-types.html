What Are “AI Surfaces”?

An AI surface is any interface where artificial intelligence generates answers and recommendations using external sources.

Examples include:

  • Google AI Overviews

  • Google AI Mode

  • ChatGPT

  • Perplexity, Bing Copilot, and similar AI assistants

While these tools often answer the same questions, they do not rely on the same sources—or reward the same content.

That distinction matters.

AI Overviews vs AI Mode: Same Question, Different Rules

Ahrefs analyzed 730,000+ AI responses and uncovered something critical:

  • AI Overviews and AI Mode share only ~13.7% of citations

  • In other words, 87% of their sources are different

  • Yet their answers are 90% semantically similar

Think of it like two experts giving the same advice—one cites research papers, the other cites real-world case studies. Same conclusion. Different trust signals.

What this tells us:

  • AI does not think in keywords

  • AI thinks in concepts, entities, and topical relationships

Why This Breaks Traditional SEO Thinking

For years, SEO taught businesses to:

  • Optimize a page

  • Rank a keyword

  • Win traffic

AI search changes the goalpost.

Now the goal is:

Be recognized as a trusted entity across multiple AI surfaces

That requires content diversity, not just optimization.

The AI Surfaces vs Content Types Framework

Here’s the framework we use to explain what each AI surface prefers—and why a single content strategy no longer works.

🟥 AI Overviews

What it trusts:

  • YouTube

  • Reddit

  • Video content

  • Strong core website pages

Best content types:

  • Explainer videos

  • Community-driven discussions

  • Clear, concise service pages

  • “Best of” and summary-style content

Why:
AI Overviews are designed to give fast, confident answers. They prioritize clarity, popularity, and engagement signals.

🟦 AI Mode

What it trusts:

  • Wikipedia

  • Quora

  • Health and authority sites

  • Encyclopedic resources

Best content types:

  • In-depth guides

  • Well-cited educational content

  • Definitions, processes, and frameworks

  • Entity-rich pages

Why:
AI Mode behaves more like a research assistant. It rewards completeness and credibility over brevity.

🟩 ChatGPT

What it trusts:

  • Broad web crawl data

  • Q&A patterns

  • Conceptual consistency

Best content types:

  • Thought leadership articles

  • Clear explanations

  • Story-driven insights

  • Structured FAQs

Why:
ChatGPT synthesizes patterns across the web. It surfaces brands that explain topics well – not just those that rank.

🟪 Perplexity & Copilot

What they trust:

  • Research papers

  • Academic sources

  • Recent news

  • High-authority citations

Best content types:

  • Whitepapers

  • Case studies

  • Data-backed content

  • Press and expert commentary

Why:
These tools emphasize verifiability. They want sources that look authoritative even under scrutiny.

The Big Shift: From Keywords to Topical Authority

One of the most important insights from the Ahrefs study is this:

Exact phrase overlap matters less than semantic relevance.

That means:

  • You don’t need to match the question word-for-word

  • You do need to fully cover the topic

This is why topical authority beats page-level optimization in AI search.

What Businesses Should Do Now

Here’s the practical takeaway:

  1. Track AI Overviews and AI Mode separately
    They are different channels with different rules.

  2. Stop assuming one AI win equals total AI visibility
    It doesn’t.

  3. Build content ecosystems, not isolated pages
    Blogs, videos, FAQs, guides, citations—all matter.

  4. Optimize for entities and topics, not just keywords
    AI rewards brands that “own” a subject.
