Traditional search is gone.

People are no longer typing:

“Who is the best consulting company for my situation and why?”

That shift matters because:

  • AI now answers questions directly
  • Users often never scroll past the AI answer
  • Google is pushing AI Overviews to the top of results

👉 Over 50% of searches already trigger AI results, and that number is expected to exceed 70% soon

What AI Actually Looks For

AI doesn’t trust what you say about yourself.

It trusts what others say about you.

That means:

  • Reviews matter more (across multiple platforms)
  • Mentions matter (articles, podcasts, social)
  • Authority signals matter (not just keywords)

👉 If your brand isn’t being talked about online, AI assumes:

“You’re not the best option.”

How AI Reads Your Content (This Is Critical)

Google used to:

  • Read content word-for-word

AI now:

  • Skims content
  • Prioritizes:
    • Summaries
    • Direct answers
    • Clear structure

👉 If your content isn’t structured for AI, it may be ignored.
