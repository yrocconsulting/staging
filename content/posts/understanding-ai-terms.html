TL;DR

AI SEO: Optimizing your brand so AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) understand and recommend you.

AEO: Structuring content so it becomes the direct answer that search engines and AI pull into snippets and instant responses.

GEO: Getting your business mentioned inside generative AI outputs and recommendations.

SXO: Optimizing the entire search-to-conversion experience so traffic turns into leads and customers.

AI SEO (Artificial Intelligence Search Engine Optimization)

Definition:
AI SEO is the process of optimizing your website and brand so you appear in AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot, and Perplexity. It focuses on making your content and entities understandable to AI models, not just traditional search crawlers.

What it really means:
Google isn’t the only “search engine” anymore – AI assistants are giving direct answers, and your business needs to be included in those AI answers.

Key elements of AI SEO:

  • Schema markup & structured data clarity

  • Entity-based content (People, Brands, Locations, Services)

  • A “clean” knowledge graph

  • Keyword → intent → entity alignment

  • Appearing in AI Overviews

  • Ensuring AI models trust your brand as a source

Why it matters:
AI models will become the primary way people search – so if AI doesn’t understand your business, you’ll disappear.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

Definition:
AEO is the practice of optimizing your content so that AI tools and search engines can give an instant answer to the user’s question.

Think:
“How do I get the snippet? How do I become the direct answer?”

Key elements:

  • Creating answer-ready content

  • Short, direct definitions

  • FAQ blocks with schema

  • Clearly formatted explanations

  • Zero-fluff paragraphs

  • Supporting structured data

Why it matters:
Google, Bing, ChatGPT, and Perplexity are replacing search results with answers.
AEO ensures you are the answer they give.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

Definition:
GEO focuses on making your business show up inside generative AI responses, not just search results.

While AEO is about the answer snippet, GEO is about being present inside AI-generated long-form outputs.

Examples:

  • “Who are the best roofers in Texas?”

  • “Create a 3-day travel plan for Dallas.”

  • “Recommend a marketing consultant specializing in AI SEO.”

You want the AI model to mention your business as part of the output.

Key elements:

  • Strong entity presence across the web

  • High-authority citations

  • Consistency in NAP + digital footprint

  • Knowledge Panel building

  • “SameAs” connections to trusted platforms

  • Being featured in lists, directories, citations, and articles

Why it matters:
As generative AI handles more search queries, GEO makes sure your business gets recommended – not erased.

SXO (Search Experience Optimization)

Definition:
SXO is the optimization of the complete user experience from the moment they search → click → interact → convert.

It combines:

  • SEO

  • UX (user experience)

  • CRO (conversion rate optimization)

Purpose:
Not just to get traffic, but to make sure the traffic converts.

Key elements:

  • Clean, fast, mobile-first design

  • Clear CTAs

  • Trust signals (reviews, photos, brand authority)

  • Site architecture and navigation

  • Chat widgets, lead magnets, and frictionless paths

  • Behavioral signals (time on site, bounce, journey flow)

Why it matters:
Ranking is step one.
Converting that traffic is step two – and SXO bridges both.
