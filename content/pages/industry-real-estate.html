FAQ

AI search for real estate agents and teams: FAQs

Can AI recommend individual agents?

Yes. When people ask for the best agent in a city or neighborhood, AI assistants often name individual agents and teams that have strong, consistent local signals and reviews.

Our brokerage controls the website. Can you still help?

Yes. We can strengthen your profiles, reviews, local content and personal entity signals, and work with your brokerage site where possible.

Does market content really matter?

It is one of the clearest ways to prove local expertise. Helpful, accurate neighborhood and market content gives AI concrete reasons to recommend you.

We are a team. Should we promote the team or each agent?

Usually both, connected clearly. We help AI understand the team brand and each agent, so whichever name people ask about leads back to you.
