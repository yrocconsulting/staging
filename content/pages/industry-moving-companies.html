FAQ

AI search for moving companies: FAQs

Do people ask AI to recommend movers?

Yes, especially because of scam concerns. AI often names movers with clear licensing, honest pricing information and strong reviews.

How do we prove we are not a broker or scam?

Show your own trucks, team, licensing and physical address consistently, and explain exactly how your estimates work.

Should we publish moving cost information?

Yes. Typical price ranges and what affects them help AI answer cost questions with your company in the answer.

Can you help with long-distance moves?

Yes. We build content around the routes you serve so AI recommends you for those moves.
