Hormone Clinic: $20K in 60 Days

SEO, paid social and local marketing drove a 70% traffic surge and $20K in new revenue in 60 days.

70%Increase in Website Traffic
$20K+New Revenue Generated

A strategic SEO and digital marketing overhaul helped this New Braunfels hormone therapy clinic boost visibility, drive patient growth, and generate over $20K in new revenue within just two months.

Background

This New Braunfels medical clinic specializes in personalized hormone replacement therapy, partnering with Evexias to offer advanced treatments for hormonal balance and overall wellness. Although they had strong clinical expertise and a trusted brand, the clinic struggled to attract new patients through digital channels in an increasingly competitive healthcare market.

The Problem

Although the New Braunfels clinic had a reputation for exceptional hormone replacement therapy and patient care, it wasn’t translating into consistent online visibility or patient growth. The clinic’s website had limited organic traffic, poor keyword rankings, and lacked optimized content tailored to what patients were actively searching for. As a result, high-intent visitors, those actively looking for solutions like bioidentical hormone therapy or testosterone treatment, were finding competing clinics instead.

Additionally, the clinic’s Google Business Profile wasn’t ranking in the top 3 local results for critical terms, which meant it was missing out on valuable local search exposure. With minimal presence in search results and low digital engagement, the clinic faced declining inquiry rates and underutilized appointment slots. Despite having a high-value service offering, the lack of digital strategy was capping growth and reducing their ability to compete effectively in a market increasingly driven by online discovery and trust signals.

Competitive Market Positioning

The hormone replacement therapy space, especially in regions like New Braunfels, is filled with both local clinics and expanding national providers offering similar services. With terms like “BHRT,” “hormone therapy for women,” and “testosterone treatment near me” in high demand, patients often turn to search engines and social media to guide their healthcare decisions.

Despite offering exceptional, personalized care, the clinic struggled to stand out in this crowded digital space. Without a strong marketing presence, even top-tier providers risk being overlooked. That’s why a strategic digital overhaul, focused on visibility, credibility, and conversion, was not just helpful, but necessary to stay competitive and grow.

The Solution

To address the clinic’s digital visibility and lead generation challenges, Yroc Consulting developed a comprehensive digital marketing strategy tailored to their niche audience. The campaign began with in-depth keyword research and full SEO optimization, including website content updates, metadata improvements, and technical SEO enhancements such as faster page speeds and mobile responsiveness. The team also built high-quality backlinks from trusted sources to increase domain authority and improve rankings for hormone-related search terms.

Simultaneously, the clinic’s Google Business Profile (GBP) was optimized to improve local search visibility. This included consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) information, geo-targeted keyword implementation, and a review generation strategy to build trust and credibility in the local community. To increase immediate patient engagement, targeted Facebook ad campaigns were launched, designed to reach individuals in the New Braunfels area searching for hormone therapy solutions. These ads featured persuasive visuals, clear calls-to-action, and directed users to book appointments. Together, these efforts created a multi-channel strategy focused on driving both visibility and high-quality conversions.

Results

Within just two months, the impact of Yroc Consulting’s strategy was significant. The clinic generated an additional $20,250 in revenue and experienced a 70% increase in website traffic. More importantly, the improved site experience and optimized messaging helped convert this traffic into action – boosting conversion rates by 50% and increasing booked appointments by 60%.

Over the next several months, these results compounded. The clinic’s Google Business Profile began ranking in the top 3 local positions for targeted keywords, and they added 70 new keywords to their overall rankings. As visibility improved and more positive reviews were collected, patient engagement and trust also grew. By month six, the clinic had reached peak performance in traffic, leads, and conversions – proving the long-term value of an integrated SEO and digital advertising strategy built specifically for healthcare growth.
