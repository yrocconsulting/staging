FAQ

Reputation management for founders and entrepreneurs: FAQs

I am raising soon. When should we start?

Now. Building credible content and coverage takes a few months, and you want it in place before investors start their diligence.

A previous company failed. Will that hurt me?

Not if it is told well. Many investors respect experience. We make sure the story includes what you learned and what you built next.

Should the focus be me or my company?

Both, connected. Early on your name often carries the company, so we strengthen both and link them clearly.

Can you help me get on podcasts?

Yes. Podcast and media appearances are one of the most effective ways to build founder authority, and we help secure them.
