FAQ

AI search for home builders: FAQs

Do buyers really research builders with AI?

Yes. AI is increasingly part of the long research process, from cost questions to which builders to consider.

We are a custom builder with no communities. Can AI recommend us?

Yes. Clear information about the areas you build in, your style, price range and past projects lets AI match you to the right buyers.

Should we publish pricing?

Starting prices or price ranges help AI answer budget questions and keep you in consideration.

How do we handle negative reviews?

We help you respond professionally and build a steady flow of positive reviews so AI sees the full picture.
