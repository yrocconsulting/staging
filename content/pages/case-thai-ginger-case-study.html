With a mission to reach more hungry customers, Thai Ginger turned to Yroc Consulting. Our AI SEO strategy helped them rise above the competition, claiming the #1 spot in ChatGPT, Google’s AI Overview, and local search results in their market.

Background

Thai Ginger has been serving the Seattle area since 1996, bringing the vibrant flavors of Thailand to Washington through fresh ingredients, bold recipes, and warm hospitality. The Factoria/Bellevue location, owned and operated by Michael and Ty, continues that tradition with an inviting atmosphere and a focus on authentic Thai cuisine. Guests come for signature dishes like Phad Thai, Panang Curry, and Crispy Garlic Chicken, paired with attentive service and a welcoming, family-style dining experience.

Despite their strong reputation and loyal local following, Thai Ginger’s digital presence didn’t fully match the quality of their in-person experience. With AI-driven search engines like ChatGPT and Google’s AI Overview reshaping how customers discover restaurants, the brand needed to modernize its visibility strategy to stay ahead of competitors in the crowded Bellevue market.

The Problem

Even though Thai Ginger had built a beloved local reputation over decades, their online visibility didn’t reflect that success. In the Factoria/Bellevue market, competition for Thai cuisine was intense, with dozens of restaurants vying for the same keywords, reviews, and map pack visibility.

While Thai Ginger’s website ranked reasonably well for certain terms, it wasn’t optimized for AI search engines like ChatGPT or Google’s new AI Overview. These platforms interpret and summarize data differently than traditional Google results, prioritizing structured information, semantic associations, and verified brand entities. Because of this, Thai Ginger often appeared inconsistently or not at all when users asked questions like “What’s the best Thai food near me?”

Their Google Business Profile (GBP) also needed refinement. Critical elements like business categories, keyword relevance, and content signals weren’t fully leveraged to improve map pack placement. Despite years of excellent reviews and customer loyalty, the restaurant wasn’t being surfaced by AI-driven systems as the go-to recommendation for local diners.

In short, Thai Ginger had an exceptional real-world presence but was being overlooked digitally. Without intervention, they risked losing local visibility to competitors who understood the new rules of AI-driven discovery.

That’s when Yroc Consulting stepped in – to bridge the gap between authentic dining excellence and AI-driven discovery.

Did You Know?

Over 70% of Google’s AI Overview results and nearly all ChatGPT restaurant recommendations do not link directly to a business’s website – unless that business is properly entity-optimized for AI.

That means even if your website ranks well on Google, AI-powered engines may never mention your brand unless they clearly understand who you are, where you’re located, and what you offer.

Yroc Consulting’s AI SEO Optimization System bridges that gap by training AI systems to recognize, trust, and promote verified local entities like Thai Ginger. The result? Your business becomes the default answer when customers ask AI for the best option nearby.

The Solution

Yroc Consulting launched a targeted AI SEO Optimization Campaign designed to make Thai Ginger visible across every modern discovery platform – Google AI Overview, ChatGPT, Perplexity AI, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, and traditional Google Search.

Our approach focused on aligning Thai Ginger’s online presence with how AI systems interpret and rank local entities. We began by rebuilding the restaurant’s digital foundation through structured data, schema markup, and entity association, ensuring Google and AI engines could accurately understand and recommend the business.

We optimized Thai Ginger’s Google Business Profile (GBP) with enhanced descriptions, refined categories, and strategically placed local keywords, helping it signal stronger intent relevance to search algorithms. Simultaneously, we refined their website content to match conversational search behavior, embedding natural language queries like “best Thai food in Factoria” that both humans and AI recognize.

To influence AI-generated results, our team implemented an AI snippet targeting strategy, training systems like ChatGPT to surface Thai Ginger when users asked open-ended or location-based dining questions. This cross-platform optimization was supported by authority-building signals such as updated imagery, reviews, and consistent data across directories.

We didn’t just improve Thai Ginger’s SEO, we engineered discoverability in the AI era, positioning the brand for maximum visibility wherever people (or AI) search.

Results

Within just a few weeks of implementing Yroc Consulting’s AI SEO Optimization System, Thai Ginger achieved complete dominance across both traditional and AI-powered search platforms in the Factoria/Bellevue, WA area.

✅ #1 Ranking in ChatGPT – Thai Ginger became the top recommended restaurant when users asked “What’s the best Thai food in Factoria, WA?”- beating out dozens of competitors.

✅ Featured #1 in Google’s AI Overview – Google’s AI-generated summaries now prominently feature Thai Ginger as the leading choice for Thai cuisine in Bellevue and Factoria.

✅ #1 Google Business Profile Listing – With refined optimization and keyword targeting, Thai Ginger took the top position in the local Map Pack, increasing visibility for high-intent diners searching nearby.

✅ #1 Organic Google Ranking – The website now holds the top spot for local organic searches, driving consistent web traffic and engagement.

Together, these wins created a powerful digital flywheel – ChatGPT recommends them, Google reinforces them, and customers choose them.

This case study is more than a ranking success – it’s proof that Yroc Consulting’s AI-driven SEO strategy bridges the gap between traditional visibility and the next generation of search. Thai Ginger now dominates where people are asking questions, not just typing keywords, cementing its reputation as the best Thai restaurant in Factoria.
