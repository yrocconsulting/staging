How customers find you now

Safety and results are the deciding questions.

Clients considering injectables or laser treatments want to know two things: is this place safe, and will I like the results? AI tries to answer both, which is why it leans on medical oversight, injector credentials, before-and-after proof and detailed reviews.

Med spas that clearly show who performs treatments, how they are supervised, what each treatment involves and what clients say afterward give AI the confidence to recommend them by name.

FAQ

AI search for med spas: FAQs

Can AI really drive bookings for a med spa?

Yes. Clients often ask AI for the best place nearby for a specific treatment, and AI names the spas with the clearest, most credible information and the strongest reviews for that service.

Should we show prices on our website?

Price ranges or starting prices help AI answer cost questions with your spa in the answer. We help you share pricing guidance without locking yourself into exact quotes.

Do before and after photos help?

They help clients decide, and clear, properly consented galleries with descriptive text support the specific treatment pages AI reads.

We have multiple locations. Can you help each one?

Yes. Each location gets consistent details, its own profile optimization and local content so AI recommends the right spa for each area.
