FAQ

AI search for apartment communities: FAQs

Do renters really ask AI where to live?

Yes, more every year. AI is asked for apartments with specific features nearby, and it names communities with clear, consistent information.

Our listing sites already have our details. Is that enough?

AI cross-checks multiple sources. Your own site, listings and reviews need to agree, or AI may describe you inaccurately.

Can you help a portfolio of communities?

Yes. Each community gets consistent data and its own local signals, managed together.

How do resident reviews affect AI?

Heavily. AI summarizes what residents say, so a steady flow of positive reviews and good responses directly shapes how it describes you.
