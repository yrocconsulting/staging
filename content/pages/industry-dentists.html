How customers find you now

Patients choose dentists on very specific needs.

People rarely search for just a dentist. They want one who takes their insurance, sees kids, offers sedation, handles emergencies or specializes in implants and cosmetic work. AI is great at matching those needs, if it has the information.

Practices that clearly list insurance, services, technology, comfort options and dentist credentials, and that have strong patient reviews, are the ones AI names for specific requests.

FAQ

AI search for dental practices: FAQs

Do patients use AI to choose a dentist?

Yes. Questions about finding a dentist who takes certain insurance or offers specific treatments are common, and AI names practices with clear information.

How do we respond to reviews without breaking privacy rules?

We help you respond in a way that never confirms someone is a patient or discusses their care.

Should we publish treatment prices?

Price ranges for elective treatments like implants or Invisalign help AI answer cost questions with your practice included.

We have several dentists. Should each have a page?

Yes. Individual dentist pages with credentials help AI recommend the practice and each provider.
