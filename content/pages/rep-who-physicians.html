FAQ

Reputation management for physicians: FAQs

How do we respond to reviews without violating privacy?

We help you respond in general, professional terms that never confirm someone was a patient or discuss their care.

Can negative reviews be removed?

Only when they break a platform's rules, such as fake or abusive reviews, and we help you report those. Otherwise we build enough genuine positive feedback to balance the picture.

I am employed by a hospital. Can I still do this?

Yes. Your personal professional reputation follows you across employers, and we work within any policies your organization has.

Does this help my practice too?

Yes. Patients often choose a practice because of a specific doctor, so a strong personal reputation supports the whole practice.
