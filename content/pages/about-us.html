Our story

Built by an entrepreneur, for business owners.

Founder Cory Long grew up in small business. In 1982 his father bought a local business in West Texas with his last paycheck in hand, and Cory helped it grow into a multi-million dollar company over more than 30 years.

He went on to rebuild and own multiple businesses, work with Fortune 500 companies and lead nonprofits, including serving as CEO of a children's home in Wichita, Kansas. Through every one of them he was fascinated by how marketing works, and how often it doesn't.

When he started a new company in North Texas in 2014, he went looking for marketers who could drive real customers, not just page-one rankings. He found that approach, mastered it, and built Yroc Consulting around it. Since then Yroc has advised more than 400 companies, and today we bring that same results-first approach to the AI search era.
