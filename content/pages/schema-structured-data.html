What it is

What is schema markup?

Schema markup is a standardized vocabulary (from schema.org) added to your website, usually as JSON-LD, that labels exactly what each page describes: who you are, what you offer, where you operate and what others say about you.

Search engines and AI systems use it to confirm identity, understand relationships and decide which facts they can trust. It doesn't guarantee visibility, but it directly reduces ambiguity, and ambiguity is what keeps businesses out of AI answers.

FAQ

Schema & structured data FAQs

Does schema directly improve AI visibility?

It reduces ambiguity, which raises the confidence AI systems have in your business. It works best combined with consistent entity data, strong content and trust signals.

Can't a plugin handle schema?

Plugins add basic markup, but it's often generic, duplicated or out of sync with your actual services. Custom schema models your real business and relationships.

Will it work on my website platform?

Yes. JSON-LD works on WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace and custom sites.

Is review schema allowed?

Yes, within search engine guidelines. We implement review markup in ways that follow those rules so it helps rather than risks penalties.
