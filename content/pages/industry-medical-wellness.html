How customers find you now

Health questions are held to a higher standard.

AI assistants are careful with health topics. They favor sources that show real medical credentials, clear and accurate information, and a strong patient reputation, and they avoid recommending practices that look vague or overpromise.

For a clinic, that means your providers, credentials, treatments, locations and patient reviews need to be clearly stated and consistent everywhere AI looks, from your website to health directories to your Google Business Profile.

FAQ

AI search for medical and wellness practices: FAQs

Is it safe to market a medical practice through AI search?

Yes, when it is done carefully. We focus on accurate information, real credentials and patient reviews, and we never publish patient details. Your team reviews clinical content before it goes live.

How do you handle reviews and patient privacy?

We help you ask for reviews in a privacy-conscious way and respond without confirming anyone is a patient or discussing their care.

Do you work with cash-pay and wellness clinics?

Yes. Medical weight loss, hormone therapy, IV therapy and other wellness practices are a strong fit, because patients research these treatments heavily before choosing a provider.

Can AI recommend a specific clinic for a treatment?

Often, yes. When someone asks where to get a treatment nearby, AI assistants regularly name local practices that have clear, consistent and well-reviewed information about that treatment.
