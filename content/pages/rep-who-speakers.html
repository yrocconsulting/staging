What planners check

Before they book, they look for…

Why it matters

Planners are risk averse. Your search results decide.

An event planner is putting their reputation on the line when they book you. They want proof you deliver and assurance there is nothing in your past that will surprise their audience or their boss.

They search Google, check speaker bureaus, watch video and increasingly ask AI to summarize who you are. A strong, consistent presence makes the decision easy. A thin or messy one sends them to the next name on the list.

Will this help me get booked more?

A strong online presence removes doubt for planners and bureaus. It does not replace a great talk, but it makes sure your search results never cost you a booking.

Can AI really affect speaking bookings?

Yes. Planners increasingly ask AI to suggest or vet speakers on a topic. Clear authority signals make you more likely to be suggested and described well.

Yes. We cannot erase legitimate news, but we build stronger, current content that tells the full story and moves the old result down.
