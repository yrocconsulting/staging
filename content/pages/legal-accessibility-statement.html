Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making yrocconsulting.com usable by everyone.

Last updated November 16, 2026

Our commitment

Yroc Consulting is committed to making our website accessible to people of all abilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, and we treat accessibility as part of how the Site is designed and built, not an add-on.

What we've done

  • Semantic page structure with clear headings and landmarks
  • Full keyboard navigation, including menus, tabs, sliders and forms, with visible focus indicators
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Color contrast chosen to support readability
  • Background video that can be paused, and that is replaced with a still image for visitors who turn on reduced motion in their device settings
  • Animations that respect the "reduce motion" setting
  • Form fields with labels, clear error messages and status updates announced to screen readers
  • Layouts that adapt to screen size and browser zoom

Known limitations

Some content comes from other services, such as embedded maps, videos and older blog articles, and may not fully meet the same standard. Interactive demonstrations on the Site (such as the AI answer preview and the entity graph) are illustrative; the same information is always available in text nearby. We review and improve the Site on an ongoing basis.

Feedback and assistance

If you have difficulty using any part of the Site, or need information in another format, please contact us. We'll do our best to respond within two business days and provide the information you need.
