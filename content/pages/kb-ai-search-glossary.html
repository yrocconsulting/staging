AI search glossary

The terms you'll hear in AI search, explained in plain English.

AI Mode
Google's conversational search experience that answers questions in depth and recommends businesses inside the answer.
AI Overviews
The AI-written summaries Google shows at the top of many search results, often citing a handful of sources.
AI Search Optimization (AISO)
Structuring a business's digital presence so AI search and answer systems can understand, trust and recommend it.
AI visibility
How often, and how favorably, your business appears in AI-generated answers for the questions your customers ask.
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
Creating content that AI assistants can extract, quote and cite directly in their answers.
Citation
A mention of your business on another website. In AI answers, a citation is a source the answer links to.
E-E-A-T
Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness and Trustworthiness: the qualities Google uses to judge content and sources.
Entity
A uniquely identifiable real-world thing: a business, person, product or place. AI systems reason about entities, not keywords.
Entity disambiguation
How AI tells similar entities apart, for example two businesses with similar names in different cities.
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
Optimizing for generative AI results such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, which write an answer instead of listing links.
Knowledge Graph
Google's database of entities and the relationships between them. It powers Knowledge Panels and informs AI answers.
Knowledge Panel
The information box Google shows for a known business or person, drawn from its Knowledge Graph.
Large Language Model (LLM)
The AI model behind tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Copilot. It generates answers from patterns learned in its training data and from sources it retrieves.
Mention
When an AI answer names your business, with or without a link.
NAP consistency
Keeping your business Name, Address and Phone identical everywhere online so AI can confidently connect every mention to you.
Prompt
The question or request a person types into an AI tool. AI visibility is measured across the prompts your buyers use.
Retrieval (RAG)
When an AI system looks up fresh sources from the web or a database before writing its answer, instead of relying only on training data.
Schema markup
Structured data added to a website (usually JSON-LD) that tells machines exactly what a page and business are.
Topical authority
Being recognized as a deep, reliable source on a subject because you cover it thoroughly and consistently.
Zero-click search
A search where the user gets an answer without visiting any website, increasingly common with AI answers.
