FAQ

AI search for professional services firms: FAQs

Do business owners really use AI to find advisors?

Increasingly, yes. Many start by asking AI what kind of help they need and who provides it, and AI often names firms with clear specialization and visible expertise.

We get most clients through referrals. Why does AI matter?

Referred prospects still check you out, and many now ask AI about you. If AI cannot confirm your expertise, or describes you inaccurately, referrals can stall.

How do we show expertise without giving away our services?

We share the what and why, the questions clients ask and how to think about them, while your hands-on work stays the service they hire you for.

Can you help our leaders build personal authority too?

Yes. Principals and partners can be connected to the firm as recognized experts, which strengthens how AI sees both.
