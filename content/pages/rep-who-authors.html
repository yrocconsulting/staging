FAQ

Reputation management for authors: FAQs

Can you get me a Google Knowledge Panel?

We build the signals Google uses to create and enrich panels, such as consistent profiles, structured data and credible mentions, and help you claim and update yours. Google makes the final decision, so no one can guarantee a panel, but we greatly improve the odds.

Can you remove bad reviews of my book?

We cannot remove honest reviews, and we would not try. We help you earn more genuine reviews, respond professionally and make sure your overall presence tells the full story.

Another author has my name. Can you help?

Yes. Clear entity signals, consistent profiles and your own site help Google and AI tell you apart.

Do I need a website if I am on Amazon?

Yes. Your own site is the one source you control, and it is often what Google and AI treat as the authoritative source about you.
