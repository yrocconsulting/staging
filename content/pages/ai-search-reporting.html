What it is

What is AI search reporting?

Traditional rank tracking tells you where a page ranks. AI search reporting tells you whether AI actually names your business when someone asks a question, on which platform, and whether it links to you as a source.

It's the same weekly tracking that powers the live client numbers on our homepage, turned into a clear report with a strategist walking you through what changed and what we're doing next.

FAQ

AI search reporting FAQs

Which AI platforms do you track?

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and Gemini.

What's the difference between a mention and a citation?

A mention is when an AI answer names your business. A citation is when it links to your website or a page about you as a source. Both matter, and we track them separately.

How often is visibility tracked?

Every week. You receive a monthly report and strategist review, with weekly data available along the way.

Can agencies white-label these reports?

Yes. Certified partners can deliver branded AI visibility reports through our white label program.
