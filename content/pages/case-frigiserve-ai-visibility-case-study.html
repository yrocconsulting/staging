FrigiServe Cold Storage is a commercial cold storage warehouse in Fort Worth, Texas, providing cooler and freezer storage, blast freezing, inventory management and distribution for food producers, retailers, foodservice and pharmaceutical customers across North Texas.

The challenge

Cold storage is a high-value, B2B decision. Buyers are food manufacturers and distributors with specific needs: blast freezing, cross-docking, inventory management and temperature-controlled storage near their routes. More and more of them start that search by asking an AI assistant, and AI answers typically name just a few providers.

FrigiServe has been a Yroc client for years. The relationship started with foundational SEO services in San Angelo, where the company's roots are. As the business grew, it built a new cold storage warehouse in Fort Worth and needed to win customers in a much larger, more competitive market: Dallas–Fort Worth.

That meant being found by food manufacturers and distributors who had never heard of FrigiServe, at the exact moment they were asking AI where to store and freeze their products.

What we did

Using the Yroc Method, we focused on the signals AI uses to identify and trust a specialized B2B provider:

  • Answer-first website content that states clearly what FrigiServe does, where and for whom, including FAQs written the way buyers ask questions.
  • Service and audience pages for freezing and blast freezing, inventory management, and the producers, retailers and foodservice providers they serve.
  • Entity consistency so FrigiServe's name, Fort Worth location and services match across its website, business profiles and directories.
  • Structured data describing the business, services and location in a format AI systems read first.

The results

In the week of November 16, 2026, FrigiServe appeared in 47 AI answers across 5 tracked buyer prompts, with 90% AI visibility. ChatGPT named FrigiServe 38 times and Gemini 9 times, and AI answers linked to FrigiServe as a source 20 times.
