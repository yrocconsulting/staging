Questions, answered

AI search, in plain English.

What is AI search optimization?

AI search optimization is the work of making your business easy for AI platforms — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google's AI Overviews — to identify, trust and recommend. It combines entity optimization, structured data, authority building, reputation and answer-ready content.

Is this different from traditional SEO?

Yes. Traditional SEO focuses on ranking pages in a list of links. AI search produces a single answer, often naming only a few businesses. Rankings still matter, but AI also weighs how consistently your brand appears across the web, what others say about you, and how clearly it can connect you to a service and location.

Do I still need SEO?

Generally, yes. If you want customers to find you, SEO is still the foundation — it drives organic traffic and qualified leads. AI optimization builds on top of it so you're visible both in classic search results and in AI-generated answers.

How long until AI starts recommending my business?

It depends on your starting point and competition. Many clients see measurable changes in how AI describes them within the first few months, as entity and authority signals are corrected and strengthened. Your AI Visibility Audit gives you a realistic roadmap.

Yes. Agencies certified through the AI Authority Engine can offer our services under their own brand through our white label program.
