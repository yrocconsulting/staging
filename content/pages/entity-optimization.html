What it is

What is entity optimization?

An entity is a uniquely identifiable real-world thing: your business, your founder, your locations, your services. AI systems don't evaluate keywords. They evaluate entities, and they only recommend the ones they can confidently identify.

Entity optimization aligns every signal about your business across your website, Google Business Profile, directories, social profiles, structured data and knowledge sources, so AI builds one clear, trustworthy picture of who you are.

FAQ

Entity optimization FAQs

What is an entity in AI search?

A uniquely identifiable real-world thing such as a business, person, place or product. AI systems build internal representations of entities and recommend the ones they understand and trust.

Is this the same as local SEO citations?

It overlaps, but goes further. Citations are one input. Entity optimization also covers structured data, knowledge sources, relationships between people and services, and how AI disambiguates you from similar businesses.

How long does it take?

Core alignment usually happens in the first weeks. AI systems update at different speeds, so how they describe you improves over the following months.

Do personal brands need entity optimization?

Yes. Founders, doctors, attorneys and other professionals are entities too. A clear personal entity helps AI connect you to your expertise and your business.
