AI search for restaurants and caterers: FAQs

Do people really use AI to choose a restaurant?

Yes. Questions like the best tacos near me or where to take a client for dinner are common, and AI answers name specific restaurants.

Our menu is a PDF. Is that a problem?

It can be. Menus as text on your website are much easier for AI to read and match to dishes people ask about. We help you publish yours properly.

Can you help with catering as well?

Yes. Catering gets its own content and signals so AI can recommend you for events, offices and celebrations.

How do reviews affect AI recommendations?

AI uses reviews to understand what you are known for. Reviews that mention specific dishes and experiences help it recommend you for those exact requests.
