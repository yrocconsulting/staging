Negative press buried his brand. Ninety days later, Ron Fossum Jr. owned Page One – with a verified Knowledge Panel, national media features, and total control of his online narrative.

Background

Ron Fossum Jr. is a nationally recognized financial strategist, author, and fractional CFO known for helping business owners build wealth and reduce taxes through advanced financial strategies. Despite his expertise and decades of professional credibility, his online presence didn’t reflect his accomplishments. Key search results were outdated and misleading, overshadowing his positive impact in the financial education space. Ron needed a digital footprint that represented his real authority – one that aligned with the trust he’d built offline.

The Problem

When Ron first approached Yroc Consulting, his Google search results painted a distorted picture of who he was. The first page was dominated by outdated press articles, including an SEC page that no longer reflected his current business activities or professional reputation. These results carried significant weight – showing up before his company, before his professional content, and before any mention of his true expertise.

Potential clients, partners, and event organizers who searched his name were being met with unmerited negative and contextless headlines, leading to lost opportunities and a narrative beyond his control. His digital footprint lacked foundational credibility markers: there was no verified Google Knowledge Panel, no high-ranking personal website, and no consistent authority signals across the web.

This situation wasn’t simply about image – it directly impacted trust and visibility. Search engines prioritize relevance and authority, meaning those negative results would continue to dominate until strong, positive assets replaced them. Without intervention, Ron risked long-term reputational damage and continued loss of confidence from audiences who encountered misleading information first. The challenge was clear: take back control of the narrative by pushing down the outdated content, building new authoritative signals, and ensuring Google recognized the truth about who Ron is and what he represents.

Did You Know?

Over 90% of people research a person or business online before making a purchasing or partnership decision. If they don’t like what they see on Page One, nearly half will walk away – often without ever reaching out.

Reputation Fact:

A single negative result on the first page of Google can cost a business up to 70% of potential leads. That’s why building and protecting your digital narrative isn’t optional – it’s essential.

The Solution

Yroc Consulting deployed a three-phase Reputation Recovery Plan designed to rebuild authority and secure long-term control of Ron’s digital presence.

In Phase 1, our team developed a new SEO-optimized personal website, enhanced his LinkedIn profile, launched a YouTube channel, and implemented structured data through Wikidata, schema markup, and Google Books entries to trigger a verified Knowledge Panel. Simultaneously, we launched an aggressive off-page SEO strategy – publishing national press releases across Yahoo Finance, MSN, and MarketWatch, and acquiring backlinks to boost authority signals.

In Phase 2, we shifted focus to content depth and credibility reinforcement. This included publishing additional long-form articles, securing over ten national media placements, and building more than fifty high-authority backlinks to solidify the positive search results. Social proof was expanded through BBB, TrustPilot, and other review platforms, while consistent LinkedIn engagement further strengthened credibility.

Finally, Phase 3 established long-term maintenance to ensure the results would last. Ongoing content production, quarterly PR campaigns, and structured link-building kept Ron’s positive assets ranking high and prevented old or negative results from resurfacing.

Results

Within just 90 days, Yroc Consulting successfully suppressed over 80% of negative search results and secured a verified Google Knowledge Panel for Ron Fossum Jr. His branded assets – including his website, LinkedIn, and Tax Plan Wealth content – now dominate Page One. The SEC page dropped off the first page entirely, replaced by authoritative results that accurately reflect his professional expertise. Over the following months, we published more than 10 national media features and built 50+ high-quality backlinks, reinforcing his online authority and entity strength.

Today, Ron’s digital presence mirrors his real-world credibility. His name now connects to trusted media sources, professional achievements, and verified platforms – effectively rewriting his narrative and establishing lasting control over how he’s represented online.
