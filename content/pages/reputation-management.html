FAQ

Reputation management FAQs

Can you remove negative results completely?

Sometimes. Content that violates a platform's policies, or certain legal and privacy cases, can be removed. Most of the time the most reliable approach is suppression: building stronger positive results that outrank the negative ones.

How long does reputation management take?

Many clients see meaningful movement within 60 to 90 days. Timelines depend on how strong the negative results are and how much positive authority already exists.

Does this affect what ChatGPT and AI Overviews say?

Yes. AI answers draw on the sources that rank and the entities they can verify. Improving those sources improves how AI describes you.

Is my inquiry confidential?

Completely. Reputation work often involves sensitive situations, and we treat every consultation and engagement as confidential.

Do you work with individuals as well as businesses?

Yes. We work with executives, professionals and public figures through Personal Reputation Management, and with companies of every size.
