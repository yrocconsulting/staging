How customers find you now

Legal research starts with a question, not a firm name.

AI chooses those firms by looking at practice area clarity, attorney credentials, bar standing, case experience, legal directory profiles and client reviews. Firms that explain their practice areas clearly and consistently are far easier for AI to recommend.

FAQ

AI search for law firms: FAQs

Can AI recommend a specific law firm?

Yes. When people ask for a lawyer for a specific type of case in their area, AI assistants often name firms with clear practice area information, credentials and strong reviews.

How do you handle attorney advertising rules?

We write educational content, avoid guarantees and claims about results, and your attorneys approve anything sensitive before it is published. Rules vary by state, so your firm has the final say.

We practice in several areas. Is that a problem?

Not if each area is explained clearly. We give every practice area its own strong page and signals, so AI does not have to guess what you handle.

Do legal directories still matter?

Yes. AI cross-checks your firm against trusted legal directories, so complete and consistent profiles there make you easier to recommend.
