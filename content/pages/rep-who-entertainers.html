FAQ

Reputation management for entertainers: FAQs

Can you fix my Wikipedia page?

Wikipedia has strict rules against paid editing, so we never edit it on your behalf. We help build the independent, reliable coverage editors rely on, and correct errors in sources we can influence properly.

My bio on Google is wrong. Can you fix it?

Yes. We correct the sources Google draws from, help you claim your Knowledge Panel and suggest edits directly.

Do you work with independent artists?

Yes. Independent artists often benefit most, because a strong presence helps them stand out without a label's publicity team.

Can you help with a career change or rebrand?

Yes. We help you reposition your name for a new genre, role or direction while keeping your history in context.
