Why it matters

Trust is the whole sale.

Coaching and consulting are bought on trust. Prospects cannot try you before they buy, so they look for proof: credentials, results, testimonials, media and what others say about you.

They also ask AI whether you are worth it. If your presence is thin, or a single complaint dominates, AI may hedge or suggest someone else. A strong, consistent reputation does the selling for you.
