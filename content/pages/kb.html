Glossary

The language of AI search.

AI Mode
Google's conversational search experience that answers questions in depth and recommends businesses inside the answer.
AI Overviews
The AI-written summaries Google shows at the top of many search results, often citing a handful of sources.
AI Search Optimization (AISO)
Structuring a business's digital presence so AI search and answer systems can understand, trust and recommend it.
AI visibility
How often, and how favorably, your business appears in AI-generated answers for the questions your customers ask.
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
Creating content that AI assistants can extract, quote and cite directly in their answers.
Citation
A mention of your business on another website. In AI answers, a citation is a source the answer links to.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is AI search replacing Google?

Not replacing, but changing it. Google itself now answers many searches with AI Overviews and AI Mode, and more people ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity directly. Businesses need to be visible in both classic results and AI answers.

How do I know if AI recommends my business?

Ask the AI tools the questions your customers ask and see who they name. For a thorough answer across every major platform, request an AI Visibility Audit.

Does traditional SEO still matter?

Yes. AI systems often draw on the same sources that rank well in search. AI search optimization builds on SEO by strengthening entity clarity, authority and trust.

What is the fastest way to improve AI visibility?

Fix the foundation first: consistent business information everywhere, clear service pages, schema markup and a steady flow of genuine reviews. Those signals reduce AI's uncertainty about you.
