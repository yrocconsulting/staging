Audit findings

Common problems we uncover.

Most businesses aren't losing visibility because they're bad companies. Their footprint is incomplete, inconsistent, or not structured for how AI evaluates trust.

  • The website doesn't clearly explain services, locations or ideal customers.
  • Business information is weak or inconsistent across profiles and directories.
  • Not enough third-party trust signals confirm the company's authority.
  • Key service pages are missing, thin, outdated or written only for keywords.
  • Schema markup is missing, incomplete, duplicated or misaligned with page content.
  • Reviews don't support the services the business wants to be known for.
  • AI recognizes competitors more clearly because they have stronger public signals.
  • No clear entity relationship between the website, profiles, founders, services and locations.

FAQ

AI Visibility Audit FAQs

What is an AI Visibility Audit?

A review of how your business appears across AI tools, search engines, answer engines, local results, business profiles, reviews, website content, structured data and trusted third-party sources.

Why does my business need one?

You may not appear in AI recommendations because AI can't clearly understand, verify or trust your online presence. The audit identifies those gaps.

Which platforms do you review?

ChatGPT-style answers, Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot, Perplexity, traditional Google search, local search, business profiles and third-party authority sources.

Does it include a schema review?

Yes. Structured data helps search engines and AI understand your organization, services, locations, FAQs and page purpose.

Will it tell me why competitors show up instead of me?

Yes. We compare content, reviews, citations, profiles, schema, authority signals and entity clarity against the competitors AI is recommending.

Is this only for local businesses?

No. It helps local, regional, national and expert-led businesses, though local businesses often benefit most because AI frequently answers location-based questions.

Can Yroc fix the issues the audit finds?

Yes, through AI Search Optimization, Entity Optimization, Schema, AI Content Optimization, AI Search Reporting, local authority and reputation work.
