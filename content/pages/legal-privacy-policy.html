Privacy Policy

How Yroc Consulting, LLC collects, uses and protects information when you use yrocconsulting.com.

Last updated November 16, 2026

Who we are

Yroc Consulting, LLC ("Yroc," "we," "us") operates yrocconsulting.com (the "Site"). This policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form on the Site (for example a contact request, an AI Visibility Audit request or a strategy call request) we collect the information you enter, which may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your business name, website and city or market
  • Your preferred meeting time and type
  • Any message or details you choose to include

If you enter a business name and city in the "See what AI says" preview on our homepage, that preview runs in your browser and is not stored by us unless you go on to submit an audit request.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server logs, including IP address, browser type, pages requested and the date and time of each request. We use these logs to keep the Site secure, prevent abuse (including spam form submissions) and fix problems.

Cookies

The public pages of the Site do not use advertising cookies. WordPress may set cookies needed for logged-in administrators, and our hosting provider may set a cookie used for performance and security. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy first. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How we use information

  • To respond to your request, schedule calls and provide the services you ask about
  • To send you a confirmation of your request
  • To operate, secure and improve the Site
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

How information is stored and shared

Form submissions are stored in our website's administration area and sent to our team by email. We share information only with service providers who help us run our business, such as our website host and email provider, and only as needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.

Third-party content

Some pages include content from other services, such as an embedded Google Map on our contact and about pages and YouTube videos in some articles. When you view that content, those providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies. Our reviews section displays reviews published on Trustindex, Clutch and Trustpilot; links to those sites are governed by their policies.

Data retention

We keep form submissions for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any resulting business relationship, and then delete them unless we are required to keep them longer.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you submitted through the Site by contacting us using the details below. You can unsubscribe from any marketing email using the link in that email.

Children's privacy

The Site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
