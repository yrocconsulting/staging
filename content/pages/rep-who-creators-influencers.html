FAQ

Reputation management for creators and influencers: FAQs

Can you get me verified?

We cannot grant platform verification, but we build the press, knowledge panel and consistent profiles that platforms and Google look for, which improves your chances.

Someone is impersonating me. Can you help?

Yes. We help identify fake accounts, report them, and strengthen your official presence so fans and brands can tell what is real.

I had a controversy. Can it be erased?

Usually not entirely. We help you respond, then build strong, current content so one moment does not define your name.

Why do I need a website if my audience is on social?

Platforms change and accounts get suspended. Your own site is the one place you control, and it is what Google and AI trust most about you.
