What it is

What is AI content optimization?

AI content optimization, sometimes called answer engine optimization (AEO), is the practice of writing and structuring content so AI systems can easily understand it, extract it and cite it when answering questions.

It's not about stuffing keywords. It's about answering the real questions your buyers ask, clearly and specifically, with the proof and context AI needs to trust and repeat your answer.

FAQ

AI content optimization FAQs

Is this the same as SEO content?

It builds on it. SEO content targets keywords and rankings. AI content optimization targets the specific questions people ask AI and the clarity and proof AI needs to cite you.

Do you use AI to write the content?

We use AI tools to research and speed up drafts, but every page is shaped and reviewed by our team so it's accurate, specific to your business and genuinely helpful.

How many pages do I need?

It depends on your services and markets. Most businesses benefit from strong individual service pages, location pages where relevant, and focused FAQ and comparison content.

Will this help my Google rankings too?

Usually, yes. Clear, specific, well-structured content tends to perform well in both traditional search and AI answers.
