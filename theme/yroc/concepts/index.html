Homepage design concepts

Three different directions for the new yrocconsulting.com. Each one is a working, interactive top-of-homepage — click around, type, scroll and drag. Pick a favorite (or mix pieces) and we'll build the whole site in that direction.

Concept A

The Answer

The homepage is an AI conversation. Visitors type their business, industry and city, and an AI answer streams back about their visibility. Editorial serif type, services shown as "footnotes" — the sources AI cites.

 Concept B

The Entity Graph

A living, draggable map of your brand's trust signals. As you scroll, scattered dots connect to "Your Brand" and an AI-confidence meter climbs from 8 to 96 — the whole service story told visually.

 Concept C

One Answer

A bold, light, magazine-style direction that breaks from "dark tech agency." Huge type, a hand-drawn circle, a ticker of real AI questions and a drag slider comparing 2015 search with today's AI answer.
